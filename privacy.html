Privacy policy

Home Automation is used only by its owner's household. Last updated 18 November 2026.

What it reads

Through Google's Smart Device Management API, with the owner's permission, it reads the household's own Nest thermostat: temperature, humidity, mode and set temperatures. It changes the mode or set temperature only when someone in the household asks it to.

Where the data goes

Nowhere else. The data is shown on the household's own dashboard, which runs on a computer in the home. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Stopping access

The owner can withdraw access at any time in Google's Nest partner connections or Google account permissions. The app then stops reading the thermostat.

Contact
